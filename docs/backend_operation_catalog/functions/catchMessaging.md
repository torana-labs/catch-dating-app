| `adminSendCatchWhatsappReply` (`purpose: supportConversation`) | Callable | Admin Support reply box on a WhatsApp conversation | `supportConversations/{id}`, `.../messages/{cwsupport_…}`, `adminAuditLogs`; reads `catchWhatsappEndpointStops` | Sends one free-form text from Catch's number to the conversation's WhatsApp sender. Support roles, App Check, 30 a minute per staff member and 5 a minute per conversation. Only inside WhatsApp's 24-hour window after the person's last message (by WhatsApp's timestamp, a minute's margin), never after STOP, and only while `CATCH_WHATSAPP_REPLIES_ENABLED` is on; outside the window it refuses (approved template messages are the unbuilt seam). The message is created as `sending` before WhatsApp is called and keyed on the client request id, so a retry returns the first outcome; a WhatsApp refusal marks it `failed`, a lost answer `unknown`, and neither is resent. The WhatsApp message id is saved, and status receipts move it to sent, delivered, read or failed. The claim and the outcome are audited, without the text. Uses the trial's pinned `CATCH_WHATSAPP_REPLY_CREDENTIAL_VERSION`/`_GRAPH_VERSION`. `adminGetSupportConversation` reports the reply mode, whether sending is possible now, why not, and when the window closes. |
| `onCatchWhatsappWebhookEventCreated` (Support) | Firestore trigger on `catchWhatsappWebhookEvents` create | Backend | `supportConversations/{id}`, `.../messages`, `catchWhatsappAutoReplies` | Inbound messages that are not nominations land in Support. A photo, voice note, video, document, sticker, location, contact or button/list reply is kept as a placeholder ("[photo]", "[voice note]", "[document: name]", the button's label); the media is never downloaded, and such messages stay blocked ingress, so they are never parsed as nominations, STOP or trial input. Reactions and system notices are skipped. A support message that is the person's first, or their first after 24 hours of silence, gets "Thanks. The Catch team will reply here." once (one claim per message), only while `CATCH_WHATSAPP_REPLIES_ENABLED` is on and `CATCH_WHATSAPP_AUTOREPLY_CREDENTIAL_VERSION` is set; it shows in the thread as Catch's automatic reply and does not stop the waiting clock. Status receipts for Support replies update their message. |
