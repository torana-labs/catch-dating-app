Meet at real events. Match after.

Catch helps you discover hosted singles events and connect with people who shared the experience. Explore event details, learn about the host, and choose an event that suits you.

After you attend and check in, your private Catch window lets you express interest in other eligible attendees. When interest is mutual, you can match and start a conversation.

Manage your profile and use in-app safety tools, including reporting and blocking, to stay in control.

Catch is free to download. Individual events may have their own ticket prices and admission rules. Event availability varies by location.
