## When to use
Use a **dot** for status or a format key, a **ring** for a step not reached, a **check** for done, a **number** for numbered steps. Set `live` on the step happening now.

## What you provide
`kind`, `size` (8, 10, 12), `tone`, `format`, `live`, `value`.

## How it adapts
A format-toned marker uses the event colour of its spotlight or screen.

## States and motion
