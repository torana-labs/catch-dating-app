Checkboxes and radio buttons with a label and an optional note, for forms.

**Registry:** `catch.checkbox` · Flutter and React: `CatchCheckboxInput` · In this system: `Catch.Check`

## When to use
Use checkboxes for independent yes/no answers and radios for one pick in a short list. For big, tappable options use a choice card.

## What you provide
`kind`, `label`, `note`, `mixed`, `error`, plus the usual input props.

## How it adapts
Night look on dark screens.

## States and motion
The tick draws in over 160 ms with a spring.
