The honest part

When another tool is the better choice.

Catch is built for nights that repeat, run by hosts who want people to come back. If that isn’t you, pick something else.

Selling a one-off concert to thousands

District or BookMyShow will put it in front of far more people.

Only booking courts for friends

Playo does that well. Add Catch when you start running socials for people you don’t know yet.

A small free group that already works

Keep the WhatsApp group. Come back when you start charging, or when newcomers stop sticking.
