How it works it out

The sums, in plain words.

  • Empty spots: spots × events × the share who don’t come.
  • What you don’t collect: if people pay at the door or later, every no-show is a held spot nobody paid for. That’s empty spots × ticket price.
  • With paid booking: people pay when they book. If they don’t come, your cancellation policy decides what’s refunded, so a no-show no longer costs you the ticket.
  • Free events: nothing to collect, but the empty spots still cost you food, space and atmosphere.
